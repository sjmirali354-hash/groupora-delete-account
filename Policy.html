Groupora Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Groupora collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages user information.

Last updated: July 2026
Information We Collect How We Use Data Data Sharing Security Account Deletion Contact

🛡️ Introduction

Welcome to Groupora. Groupora is a social and community platform that allows users to create accounts, build profiles, create or join groups, communicate with other users, upload content, follow users, and manage private communities.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Groupora mobile application and related services. By using Groupora, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Please do not use Groupora if you do not agree with this Privacy Policy.

📋 Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the features you choose to use.

Account Information Email address, username, account identifier and authentication information.
Profile Information Profile photo, biography, country, city and other information voluntarily added to a profile.
Group Information Group names, descriptions, icons, banners, categories, invitation codes, memberships and administrative roles.
Messages Messages, announcements and other content sent inside groups or private conversations.
Photos and Content Profile photos, group images, chat images and other photos voluntarily uploaded by users.
Social Connections Followers, following, friends, group memberships and interactions with other users.
Reports and Moderation Reports, blocked users, moderation actions, rejection reasons and information needed to investigate violations.
Approximate Location A country or city voluntarily selected by the user. Groupora does not require users to publicly share their precise live location.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Groupora may receive basic account information provided through Google, such as your email address, display name, profile image and Google account identifier.

Information You Choose to Provide

Users may voluntarily provide information when creating a profile, creating a group, sending a message, uploading an image, submitting a report, contacting support or using other Groupora features.

⚙️ How We Use Information

Groupora may use collected information to:

  • Create, authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • Display profiles, usernames and profile photos.
  • Allow users to create, join and manage groups.
  • Deliver group messages, private messages and announcements.
  • Upload and display user-selected photos.
  • Provide follower, following and friend features.
  • Show relevant country, city or community information.
  • Process reports and perform content moderation.
  • Detect spam, abuse, fraud and security threats.
  • Protect users and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve app performance.
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests.

🤝 How Information Is Shared

Groupora does not sell users' personal information.

Information Visible to Other Users

Depending on your profile and privacy choices, other Groupora users may be able to see information such as your username, profile photo, biography, country, city, follower information, group memberships and content you choose to post or send.

Messages and images sent in a group may be visible to the members of that group. Private messages are visible to the participants in the conversation, subject to moderation, safety and legal requirements.

Service Providers

We use trusted service providers to operate Groupora. These providers may process limited information only as needed to provide their services.

Google Firebase

Groupora may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage and related Firebase services for account authentication, database storage, uploaded content and application operation.

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In may be used to authenticate users who choose to access Groupora with their Google account.

Legal and Safety Reasons

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, prevent fraud or abuse, protect the security of Groupora, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

💬 User-Generated Content

Groupora allows users to create profiles, groups, messages, descriptions, announcements and uploaded images. Users are responsible for the content they submit.

Groupora may review, restrict, remove or preserve content when necessary for moderation, security, policy enforcement, legal compliance or investigation of reported abuse.

Users may report inappropriate users, groups, messages or content using the available reporting features.

🔐 Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures intended to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Data transmitted between the application and its service providers is transmitted using secure encrypted network connections where supported.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords and should not share account credentials with other people.

🗄️ Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Groupora services, maintain an active account, meet security requirements, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Some content may remain visible until it is deleted by the user, removed by an authorized group owner or moderator, or deleted as part of an account-deletion request.

Limited information may be retained after account deletion where required for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution or enforcement of our agreements.

🎛️ Your Choices and Rights

Depending on the available Groupora features, users may:

  • Edit their username, biography or profile photo.
  • Choose whether to add optional city or country information.
  • Choose whether to upload photos or send messages.
  • Leave groups or manage groups they own.
  • Unfollow, block or report another user.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information.
  • Request permanent deletion of their Groupora account and associated data.

🗑️ Account and Data Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your Groupora account and the personal data associated with it.

Use the account-deletion page below and submit your request using the email address connected to your Groupora account.

Open Account Deletion Page Email Deletion Request
A verified deletion request may delete your profile, email address, username, profile photo, groups, memberships, messages, uploaded content, social connections and other account-associated information.

We may ask you to confirm account ownership before processing a deletion request. Verified deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days.

👥 Age Requirements

Groupora is not directed to young children. Users must meet the minimum age requirements applicable in their country and comply with the age restrictions displayed on the Google Play store listing.

If we learn that information was collected from a user who was not legally permitted to use the service, we may restrict the account and delete the related information where appropriate.

🌍 International Data Processing

Groupora and its service providers may process or store information on servers located outside the user's country. Where required, reasonable safeguards are used for the handling of such information.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Groupora adds features, changes its services, uses new service providers or needs to comply with legal and policy requirements.

The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
