🛡️ Overview

Groupora ("we", "our", or "the app") is operated by SJ64 Studios.

It lets you create and join groups, follow users and Channels, exchange direct and group messages, publish Channel posts, share media and temporary Stories, and manage a profile.

This Policy describes the information the current app processes, why it is processed, where it is stored, how long it may remain, and the controls available to you.

👤 Account and Profile Information

When you create or use an account, Groupora processes your Firebase Authentication identity and profile information such as email address, first and last name, username, bio, profile link, profile photo, gender, country, account timestamps, and verification or account-status fields.

If you sign up with Google, Google and Firebase provide the identity information needed to sign you in.

Country may be inferred from the device timezone during signup; Groupora does not use that process as GPS tracking.

We use this information to create the account, show profiles and connections, enforce account controls, and support sign-in, password changes, and account recovery.

Profile data is stored in Firebase Authentication and Firestore.

You can edit the profile fields the app exposes, hide your profile image, and control who may start a new message where the app provides that setting.

👥 Connections, Groups, and Channels

Groupora processes follow records and derives a Friend relationship when two users follow each other.

It also processes Channel follows separately from user follows.

Group records may include owners, administrators, members, join requests, invite activity, approval settings, sharing/posting settings, restrictions, and membership timestamps.

Channel records may include the owner, posts, followers, post views, reactions, and Discover metadata.

This information is used to show feeds and Discover results, deliver features to the right participants, count or display engagement, and enforce group and Channel permissions.

You can unfollow, leave groups when allowed, cancel or manage requests where available, block users, and use available group privacy and messaging controls.

Private Group Conversations

Publicly discoverable group or Channel metadata does not make private group conversations public.

💬 Messages, Media, Location, and Stickers

Direct-message and group-chat records may contain message text, sender and conversation identifiers, timestamps, replies and quoted text, reactions, delivery/read state, message previews, and sticker metadata.

Selected images and videos may be uploaded to Firebase Storage and linked from the message; the current chat uploader accepts images and videos up to 50 MB.

Group and Channel features can also use image/video, location, and sticker content where the relevant composer offers it.

Location Sharing

When you choose Share Location, the app converts the current location into a Google Maps URL containing coordinates and sends that URL as message text; recipients or group members may see it.

The current implementation does not access your contact book or provide contact-book sharing, and the chat media path does not upload audio recordings.

Custom stickers may include image or video files, title, price, dimensions, duration or trim information, creator/owner identifiers, and storage URLs.

We process this data to upload, display, share, validate, and deliver the content you choose.

Server copies are stored in Firebase Storage and Firestore.

You can delete custom stickers you own where the app provides that control.

Other users may retain received or downloaded copies.

📸 Stories, Views, and Reactions

Stories may contain text, emoji, images, or videos, together with creation and expiry timestamps, media metadata, views, and likes.

Stories are available to the author and current mutual Friends under the app's rules.

Story video uploads are limited to 60 seconds.

Stories are intended to expire after their stated period, normally within 24 hours; the server cleanup process may take time to complete.

You can delete your own Story, and you can hide a person's Stories locally on your device without changing shared Story data.

Story owners may see permitted viewer and reaction information.

⚙️ How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure accounts.
  • Show profiles, follows, Friends, groups, Channels, posts, and Discover results.
  • Deliver messages, media, stickers, reactions, receipts, and notifications.
  • Operate group roles, approvals, restrictions, Channel ownership, and follows.
  • Calculate views and engagement.
  • Maintain daily check-in, coin, referral, and approved group-promotion records.
  • Respond to reports.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Troubleshoot.
  • Enforce the Terms.
  • Meet legal obligations.
Advertising and Analytics

We do not sell personal information and did not find advertising or general analytics collection in the current app implementation.

🔔 Notifications and Device Information

If you grant notification permission, Groupora collects and stores a device push token, platform information, and update time under your user record so Firebase/Google notification services can deliver alerts.

Notification records can contain a sender name, short message preview, conversation or message identifiers, reply suggestions, read state, and timestamps.

Notifications may appear as an alert, banner, list item, sound, or badge.

You can decline system notification permission and can use the notification and conversation controls provided by the app.

A small local retry queue may retain group notification identifiers until delivery succeeds or the retry entry is removed.

📱 Local Storage and Offline Data

Groupora stores some information locally on your device for sign-in persistence, preferences, and offline convenience.

This may include:

  • Theme and language choices.
  • Archived or hidden conversations.
  • Favorites.
  • Wallpapers.
  • Translations.
  • Recent sticker IDs.
  • Locally hidden Stories.
  • Public profile snapshots.
  • Cached media.

Authorized conversation snapshots may include full message data, including text, sender/reply data, stickers, and media references; the current limits are up to 200 direct-message messages and 100 group messages per cached conversation.

A local message outbox may retain up to 200 text messages and their delivery/retry state until acknowledged, canceled, or cleared.

Downloaded Channel media is stored in the app's durable documents area, while some viewer files use temporary app cache storage.

Local Data Is Not Authoritative

Local data is for convenience only. Firebase, the API, and server-side rules remain authoritative.

A cache is never proof of authorization, membership, ownership, admin status, a purchase or entitlement, a block state, or any other security decision.

A later server change may remove access even if old data remains on the device.

Logout clears the current public-profile cache but does not currently erase every local chat, outbox, media, wallpaper, translation, sticker, or preference record.

You can clear individual conversations or device/app data where the operating system or app control provides it; cached files may otherwise remain until replaced, cleared, or removed by the device.

🛡️ Moderation, Reports, and Access

Reports may contain the reporter's user ID and name, the reported Channel or post ID, Channel/post metadata, a selected reason, details, status, and timestamps.

Eligible ordinary users can report Channel or Channel-post content where the report control is shown.

Channel owners and global admins do not receive the normal user-facing report option inside a Channel.

The Admin Panel is the reports-list surface; ordinary users and Channel owners cannot access that list.

Administrators can review and change report status, moderate available group content, manage restrictions, block accounts, and delete Channels through the authorized admin tools.

Report Retention

Reports are not automatically deleted on a fixed schedule in the current implementation and may remain for moderation, safety, or legal purposes.

🗄️ Retention and Deletion

We retain server data while it is needed to provide the feature, maintain security, process moderation, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with law.

Chat Auto-Delete

Chat auto-delete can be configured per direct or group conversation to 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, or Off; the implemented default is 90 days.

When enabled, server cleanup removes eligible message and reaction documents, but it does not guarantee removal of Firebase Storage media, downloaded files, or copies saved by other people.

Clear Chat for Me only hides older shared messages for the requesting user and does not delete them for other participants.

Coins, referral records, promotion records, reports, receipts, and engagement records have no general automatic expiry in the current implementation.

Account Deletion

Account deletion requires recent password verification for the current email/password flow.

The server schedules permanent deletion 30 days after the request; you can cancel during that recovery period by signing in and verifying your password.

At cleanup, Groupora deletes the Firebase Auth account, the Firestore user record and related account references, owned group trees, authored messages and related receipts/reactions, and user-owned Story, custom-sticker, and shared-media Storage prefixes.

Information That May Remain

Shared conversation copies, recipients' downloads, backups, limited moderation records, or records required by law may remain where the implemented deletion path does not remove them or retention is necessary.

Server cleanup runs periodically, so the deletion may complete after the scheduled time rather than instantly.

🔐 Security and Authority

Firebase Authentication, Firestore rules, Firebase Storage authorization, and the authenticated API are the authoritative security layers.

We use authentication, server checks, scoped reads, and administrative controls intended to limit access to authorized users.

Important Security Notice

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Local data, cached role information, client-side coin values, or cached content must never be treated as proof of authorization, membership, ownership, admin role, purchase or entitlement, block state, or another security decision.

🌍 Service Providers and International Processing

The current app uses Firebase services provided by Google for Authentication, Firestore database storage, Firebase Storage media storage, and related server operations.

Push delivery uses Expo notification APIs and Firebase/Google messaging services.

Google sign-in is available, and a chosen location message uses a Google Maps URL.

Support buttons open a Gmail compose link but do not give Groupora access to your mailbox.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Region

The project's exact data-region configuration was not confirmed by the app code.
