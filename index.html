Delete Your Groupora Account

Request permanent deletion of your Groupora account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Groupora account through the account deletion option available in the app's Account Settings.

If the email deletion request method is available to you, you may also send a deletion request from the email address registered with your Groupora account.

  1. Use the email address registered with your Groupora account.
  2. Use the subject: Groupora Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your registered email address and Groupora username.
  4. Send your request to: easygroupjoin@gmail.com
Request Account Deletion
Email/Password Account Verification:

For accounts using email/password authentication, the current deletion flow requires recent password verification before the deletion request can be scheduled.

30-Day Deletion Recovery Period

Account deletion is not immediate. Once a valid deletion request is confirmed, permanent deletion is scheduled for 30 days later.

During this 30-day recovery period, you may cancel the scheduled deletion by signing in to your Groupora account and completing the required password verification.

Important:

If you cancel the deletion request during the recovery period, your account will not be permanently deleted through that scheduled deletion request.

Data Removed During Account Deletion

When the scheduled deletion is processed, Groupora's server-side deletion process removes applicable account data, which may include:

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • User profile and related account references
  • Account-associated profile information
  • Owned groups and related group trees
  • Messages authored by the user
  • Related message receipts and reactions
  • User-owned Stories
  • User-created custom stickers
  • User-owned shared-media storage

Data and Copies That May Remain

Deleting your account does not necessarily remove every copy of information that has already been shared with or downloaded by other users.

Depending on how the information was shared and stored, the following may remain:

  • Copies of messages or media already received by other users
  • Files downloaded by other users
  • Shared conversation history that is not removed by the deletion process
  • Backups that have not yet expired or been cleaned up
  • Limited moderation or safety records
  • Records that must be retained to comply with applicable law
  • Information necessary for fraud prevention, security, disputes, or enforcement of our Terms of Service
Important:

Deletion of your Groupora account does not guarantee that information previously copied, downloaded, cached, or otherwise retained by another person will be removed from that person's device or storage.

Groups, Messages, Stories and Shared Content

If you own groups, the account deletion process may remove your owned group trees and related server data as described by the current service deletion process.

Messages, Stories, custom stickers, uploaded media, receipts, reactions, and other user-owned content may be removed when the associated deletion process is completed.

However, content that has already been received, copied, downloaded, cached, or otherwise retained by other users may remain outside of the deleted account.

Deletion Processing Time

A verified deletion request is scheduled for permanent deletion approximately 30 days after confirmation.

The server-managed cleanup process may take additional time after the scheduled deletion date to complete all applicable deletion operations.

Can I Cancel My Deletion Request?

Yes. For the current email/password deletion flow, you can cancel the scheduled deletion during the 30-day recovery period by signing in and completing the required password verification.

Data Retention

Groupora may retain limited information when retention is necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, moderation, or enforcement of our Terms of Service.

Such retained information is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and may remain for the period required by the relevant circumstances or applicable law.

Permanent Deletion

Warning:

Once the scheduled account deletion has been completed, it may not be possible to recover your account, profile, groups, messages, Stories, custom stickers, uploaded content, or other deleted account data.
