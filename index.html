Delete Your Groupora Account

Request permanent deletion of your Groupora account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email using the email address registered with your Groupora account.
  2. Use the subject: Groupora Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your registered email address and Groupora username.
  4. Send your request to:

Email: easygroupjoin@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion
Important: Deleting your account is permanent. After deletion you may permanently lose access to your profile, groups, messages, uploaded content, followers, following list and other account related information.

Data That May Be Deleted

  • Account profile
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture
  • Groups
  • Messages
  • Uploaded images
  • Followers and Following
  • Other account related information

Data That May Be Retained

Some limited information may be retained when required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security, or enforcement of our Terms of Service.

Processing Time

Verified deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days.
