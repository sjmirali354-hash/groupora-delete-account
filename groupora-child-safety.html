Groupora Child Safety Standards

Our commitment to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Our Commitment

Groupora is committed to maintaining a safer online environment for its users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, child trafficking, or any other content or behavior that endangers children.

Groupora prohibits the use of the service to create, upload, share, request, promote, distribute, or facilitate child sexual abuse material or the sexual exploitation of minors.

Age Requirement

Groupora is not directed to children under 13. Users must meet the minimum age requirement applicable to their use of the service and must not misrepresent their age to bypass age-related restrictions.

Child Safety Rules

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Groupora:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming or attempting to groom a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual exploitation of children or minors.
  • Child trafficking or content facilitating the exploitation of children.
  • Soliciting, requesting, offering, or distributing sexual material involving minors.
  • Encouraging or facilitating harm, abuse, or exploitation of children.
  • Using Groupora to establish contact with a minor for unlawful sexual purposes.
  • Attempting to bypass blocks, restrictions, reports, or other safety controls.

Safety Measures

  • Users can block accounts where the blocking feature is available.
  • Users can report eligible Channels or Channel posts when reporting controls are available in the app.
  • Reports may be retained for safety, moderation, security, and legal purposes.
  • The Groupora Safety & Moderation Team may review submitted reports and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.
  • Accounts that violate Groupora's Terms of Service or child safety requirements may be restricted, suspended, or permanently terminated.
  • Content that violates applicable laws or Groupora's policies may be removed or restricted when identified.
  • Where required or permitted by applicable law, Groupora may cooperate with appropriate law enforcement or relevant authorities.
Zero-Tolerance Policy:

Any account reasonably determined to be involved in child sexual exploitation, grooming, CSAM, or other serious child-safety violations may be permanently suspended or terminated and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required or permitted by applicable law.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior that may involve child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or another serious child safety concern, please use the available reporting controls within the Groupora app or contact our Safety & Moderation Team directly.

When contacting us, please provide enough information to help us identify the relevant account, group, Channel, post, or other content. Do not send or redistribute suspected illegal child sexual abuse material.

Report Review

Reports submitted through available reporting features may be reviewed by the Groupora Safety & Moderation Team. The team may take action including restricting accounts, removing content, applying appropriate account restrictions, or terminating accounts when warranted.

Submitting a report does not guarantee that the reported content will be removed. Groupora does not guarantee comprehensive automated or human review of every post, message, or piece of content.

Protection Against Abuse and Exploitation

Users must not use Groupora to harass, threaten, exploit, manipulate, or endanger children. Users must also not attempt to circumvent safety controls, account restrictions, blocks, membership checks, approval flows, or other access controls.

Cooperation With Authorities

Where required or permitted by applicable law, Groupora may cooperate with law enforcement authorities or other appropriate organizations in connection with suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, or other serious safety matters.
