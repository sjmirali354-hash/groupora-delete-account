Groupora Child Safety Standards

Our commitment to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Our Commitment

Groupora is committed to providing a safe online environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, exploitation, or any content that endangers children.

Safety Measures

  • Users can report inappropriate content, groups, messages and profiles.
  • Users can block abusive accounts.
  • Content reported for child safety concerns is reviewed promptly.
  • Accounts that violate our policies may be suspended or permanently removed.
  • Illegal child sexual abuse material is removed immediately when identified.
  • Where required by law, we cooperate with appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Community Rules

  • No child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • No grooming or exploitation of minors.
  • No sexual content involving minors.
  • No child trafficking or abuse related content.
  • No content encouraging harm to children.
Any account involved in child exploitation or child sexual abuse material will be permanently banned and may be reported to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

Reporting

Users can report inappropriate content directly within the Groupora app. Every report is reviewed by our moderation team.
